## 🏛️ Company Architecture & Core Business Model

### 1. The Core Innovation: Peer-to-Peer Netting vs. SWIFT
Traditional cross-border money transfers use the **SWIFT** network, hopping across multiple correspondent banks, each taking a cut and introducing 2–5 days of settlement delay and opaque foreign exchange (FX) markup.

Wise bypasses SWIFT for the vast majority of its volume by operating **domestic bank accounts in dozens of countries**:
1. When a user in Singapore sends SGD to the UK, they send SGD locally to Wise's Singapore bank account via FAST/PayNow.
2. Wise's UK bank account pays out GBP locally to the UK recipient via the Faster Payments System (FPS).
3. The money never physically crosses international borders—Wise nets balances internally between reserve pools.

---

### 2. The Four Mission Pillars
Wise evaluates every product decision and engineering investment against these 4 customer promises:
- **Price**: Moving fees toward zero.
- **Speed**: Target > 80% instant transfers (< 20 seconds).
- **Convenience**: Frictionless customer onboarding, instant KYC verification, and intuitive self-service.
- **Transparency**: True mid-market rate with zero hidden markups.

---

### 3. Wise in the Asia-Pacific (APAC) Region
- **Singapore Hub**: Major Payment Institution (MPI) licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS). Direct participant in FAST and PayNow.
- **Regional Footprint**: Direct integrations with national instant payment systems:
  - Singapore: FAST & PayNow
  - Malaysia: DuitNow (via PayNet)
  - Australia: NPP (New Payments Platform)
  - Thailand: PromptPay (mobile & citizen ID transfers + QR scan)
  - India: UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
- **Wise Platform**: Expanding B2B embedded finance partnerships across APAC banks and fintechs (e.g., DBS, Standard Chartered, Tiger Brokers, Aspire).
